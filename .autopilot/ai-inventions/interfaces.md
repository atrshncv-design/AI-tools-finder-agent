# Interfaces

- `news.section` and `news.sphereTags` carry section routing and multi-sphere labels.
- `invention_tools` is the persistent canonical catalog; news updates remain separate entities.
- `news.inventionTools` exposes catalog cards to the dashboard.
- `daily-digest.ts` renders and safely splits three Telegram sections.
- `HERMES_SUBAGENTS` defines the orchestrator's three niche search profiles.
